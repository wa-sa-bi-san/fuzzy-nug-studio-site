FuzzyNug Studio

Privacy policy · effective 29 October 2026

FuzzyNug Studio ("the app") is a private Android music app used by its two creators. This page explains what data the app uses and what it does with it.

No servers, no tracking

The app has no server of its own. It does not send your data to the developers or to anyone else, and it has no ads, analytics or tracking. Data is never sold.

Google user data

  • What is accessed: when you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for access to your Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) and reads your account's name and email address so it can show which account is connected.
  • How it is used: the app looks for (or creates) one folder called FuzzyNug Studio, with Samples, Projects, Recordings and Exports inside, and works only inside it. It lists, downloads and uploads files in that folder so you can use your samples and keep your songs. It does not open, change or delete your other files.
  • Where it goes: files travel only between your phone and your own Google Drive. Nothing is shared with the developers or any third party.
  • Storage on the phone: sign-in is handled by Google Play services on your phone. The app keeps the connected account's name and email and the folder IDs in its private storage on the phone, plus downloaded copies of samples.
  • Limited Use: FuzzyNug Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microphone and audio

The microphone or USB audio input is used only while you use the Record screen or live monitoring. Recordings, projects and exports are saved on your phone. They only leave the phone if you upload them to your Google Drive folder or share them yourself.

Removing access and data

  • In the app: Settings › Google Drive › Disconnect.
  • In your Google Account: Third-party apps & services, then remove FuzzyNug Studio.
  • Delete the FuzzyNug Studio folder in Google Drive to remove the cloud copies. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
