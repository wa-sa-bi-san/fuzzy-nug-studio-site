FuzzyNug Studio

A private music workstation app for Android, made by and for Fuzz and Nug.

What it does: drum machine with 16 pads, multitrack recording from a USB audio interface or the phone mic, clip editing, a mixer with EQ, compression, reverb and delay, and exporting songs.

Google Drive: the app can connect to Google Drive to keep a shared sample library and song files in one folder called FuzzyNug Studio.

FuzzyNug Studio is not on the Play Store and is not offered to the public.
